There's less than a month left until Christmas! When I stopped by the house, I was surprised to find it beautifully decorated. It really makes me happy when residents take the initiative to do this kind of thing on their own!

![Hallway wall with a Merry Christmas sign and a red ribbon](https://theheadquarters.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/IMG_6368-1024x768.jpg)

## New lighting and a tree hung with red ornaments in the dining room

Changing up the lighting made a difference too — pretty photogenic, if I do say so myself lol

![Dining room with a bare-branch tree hung with red ornaments](https://theheadquarters.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/IMG_6367-1-768x1024.jpg)

## The birch-style tree, plus a little one on the dining table

The birch-style tree got a stylish makeover as well.

![Small Christmas tree decorated with gold and red baubles](https://theheadquarters.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/IMG_6365-768x1024.jpg)

And there's a little tree on the dining table too.

![Handmade advent calendar hanging on the wall](https://theheadquarters.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/IMG_6370-768x1024.jpg)

## A handmade advent calendar, made by a British housemate

On the wall in front of the bathroom, there's now a handmade advent calendar!  
Pretty impressive, right? It was made by one of our British housemates!

Hope everyone has a wonderful Christmas season!  
And you young folks out there, go make some memories~! 😂
