2月に、ハウス内で時間差で咲いたら素敵だろうなと思って、河津桜と吉野桜と藤を買っていました。藤はいい感じで咲きそうだけど、葉が出てきたのに桜がぜんぜん咲かない。。。  
よく見たらなんとつぼみがぜんぶ落ちてました、、、なんでだろう、植替えがよくなかったか😅

悔しいのであたらしく仙台枝垂れ桜というものを買ってきました。  
どうしてもハウスで桜を咲かせたいのです！

写真の左と真ん中の2本が、葉ばかりが出てしまった桜。右がつぼみをふくらませている藤です。

Back in February, I bought a Kawazu cherry tree, a Yoshino cherry tree, and a wisteria, thinking it would be lovely if they bloomed one after another around the house at different times. The wisteria looks like it's about to bloom nicely, but even though the leaves have come out on the cherry trees, they're just not blooming at all...  
When I took a closer look, it turned out all the buds had fallen off, every single one... I have no idea why — maybe repotting them wasn't such a good idea 😅

I was so frustrated that I went out and bought a new one — a Sendai weeping cherry this time.  
I really, really want to see cherry blossoms bloom at the house!

In the photo, the two pots on the left and in the middle are the cherry trees that put out nothing but leaves. The one on the right is the wisteria, with its buds swelling up nicely.
