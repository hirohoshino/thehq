At The Headquarters Kobe, we like to stock a few nicer kitchen tools so anyone who loves to cook can really enjoy life here.  
Things like a BALMUDA toaster that makes bread taste amazing, a pressure cooker for melt-in-your-mouth simmered meat, a sous-vide cooker that's been getting a lot of buzz lately, and even a HUROM cold-press juicer.  
We pick up fresh ingredients at the Suidosuji shopping street and get together for house dinners, so come on by and join us 😊
